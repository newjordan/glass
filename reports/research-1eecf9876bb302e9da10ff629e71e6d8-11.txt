   2 wins/11, two-sided sign p .0654297. Rejected, not proven universally slower.
   Existing parser-equivalence test passed (1 test).

2. Byte-proven ASCII islands: prove eight printable successor bytes inside a
   mixed UTF-8 row; fixed-stride writes retain the last successor held, obey
   row/wide-cell boundaries and the original wrap/REP protocol. Same engine
   diagnostic: Unicode .889912, 0 wins/11, sign p .0009765625. Rejected.
   Existing parser-equivalence test passed (1 test).

   A preflight reconstructs the UNCHANGED terminal Unicode generator at 154x39,
   including the preceding ASCII generator's RNG consumption. Of 16,777,256
   bytes, 5,483,313 are printable ASCII in 2,008,491 spans. Spans >=8 cover

   is older/different; these terminal-input counts do not describe that payload.
   Payload SHA256 82e706b13af91d95d513b487459780f941b4a66b5e729c1fe7163bd840c1c193.

3. Masked-word UTF-8 decoder: one bounded four-byte load combines continuation
   checks, enforces E0/ED/F0/F4 scalar bounds, reconstructs one scalar. Old

   Initial 11 engine pairs: Unicode 1.011570, 7 wins, p .5488281. This weak
   initial gain did NOT reproduce: rebuilding both arms after identical test
   additions, 15 balanced pairs at scale 3/seven rounds gave .995212,
   5 wins/15, p .3017578. Rejected for no dependable target-workload gain.
   Do not pool the two executable layouts or discard the negative replication.
   Candidate and control production forms were built/frozen/hashed but were
   NOT benchmarked through a terminal and are NOT promoted.

All six workloads measured in each dataset, no simultaneous benchmark jobs.

of five/seven is an estimator, not independent samples. Host shared, pacing
and code layout can affect unchanged paths. No outliers filtered; sign tests
are exploratory, not corrected across metrics/candidates. Engine diagnostics
are NOT terminal throughput, rendered latency, RTT, or competitor results.
