INTERPRETATION / LIMITS
Ratios are recording on/off in-process throughput, NOT control/candidate engine
wins. Dispersion fails to establish negligible observer cost, a causal recording
penalty/benefit, or any correction factor. The same executable in both modes
cannot isolate compile-time effects versus the old ELF. Shared-host scheduling,
allocator/cache/setup and order effects remain confounded; no independence,
noninferiority, significance or terminal/Ghostty claim. Nanosecond representation
is not nanosecond accuracy; clock endpoints are adjacent, not identical.
No retroactive reinterpretation of rejected candidates. Future fresh screens
must predeclare the estimator and use the same recorder mode on both arms.
