New finding 1 — Global pointer indirection is a measured structural tradeoff

Candidate changes Storage<T> from Vec<Row<T>> to Vec<Box<Row<T>>>. It keeps
observable row/indexing/reflow interfaces using boxing adapters, adds no
unsafe code and moves pointers rather than full metadata during rotation.
Measured control layout: Row<Cell> size 64 align 8; Cell size 24 align 8;
Box<Row<Cell>> slot size 8 align 8. Do not infer that reduced byte traffic is
the only mechanism: allocation, cache residency, indirection and compiler
layout change together.

Next necessary action

Do not repeat global boxing/alignment as assumed solutions. An indirection
scheme confined to region/page boundaries must preserve direct Unicode row
access; its implementation and benefit are not yet proven. A compact cell/
page design with styles/extras in side storage remains a distinct structural
lead, with cursor-template, serde, reflow and ownership obligations. The
Unicode/region deficits and unresolved ASCII result require actual end-to-end
and latency gates,
not further celebration of an engine-only gain. Campaign is not complete.
