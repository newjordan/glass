## 5. New retrospective sensitivity: median pass rather than fastest pass

The actual producer bench/tput.py, lines around 94–103, uses time.perf_counter(),
selects min(runs), and computes official mb_s from that UNROUNDED fastest tuple.
It stores all three secs values rounded to 3 decimal places. Consequently the
retained secs cannot exactly reproduce official MB/s. That is a known producer
precision limit, not automatically evidence corruption.

After the mandatory score reconciliation, without a new capture, audit.py
computes a RETROSPECTIVE alternative: one median-of-three-time throughput ratio
per session, paired once per round. Internal passes are NOT added as samples.
This does not replace the frozen primary estimand, create a new independent
replication, or rescue any rejected policy/candidate.

| Selected workload | session-median ratio | W/L/rounded ties | six-workload Holm p | quantization-only median interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| ASCII | .922422422 | 9/21/0 | .085547891 | [.892121212, .953734815] |
| Unicode | .945037221 | 6/23/1 | .006947100 | [.929832061, .960484023] |
| Truecolor | 1.188888889 | 30/0/0 | .0000000111759 | [1.176795580, 1.201117318] |
| Cursor motion | 1.098591549 | 28/2/0 | .00000347197 | [1.083916084, 1.113475177] |

| TUI frames | 1.070869420 | 30/0/0 | .0000000111759 | [1.057590332, 1.084321087] |

For each observed median a,b, the time-quantization interval is
 [(b-.0005)/(a+.0005), (b+.0005)/(a-.0005)]. The interval for the series median
uses medians of those endpoints; monotonicity of median supplies this bound.
It bounds only possible 1ms rounding, NOT sampling uncertainty, host effects or
a confidence interval. All retained pass times are positive and above .0005.
Boundary-inclusive intervals are conservative for round-to-even ties.

There are 19/30 selected scroll-region rounds whose interval includes parity;
11 are recorded rounded ties. Rounded equality is not exact timing equality.
ASCII's and Unicode's entire median quantization intervals remain below one.
The qualitative selected result is unchanged: three conditional positive leads,
ASCII/Unicode not leads, scroll unresolved. Combined candidate sensitivity also
retains four conditional leads, with scroll median 1 and ASCII .961481481.
Its Unicode Holm p changes from .004293 in the primary estimand to .048374405
in this retrospective estimand: estimator choice matters even without changing
qualitative disposition. Rounded-pass sign p-values themselves remain
conditional/descriptive, not quantization-robust superiority proofs.

## 7. Codegen contradictions resolved; no invented cause

* i26 Unicode regresses although its relocation-masked print_utf8 body retains
  the same 1967 instructions. Caller/callee/layout/data-path effects remain
  hypotheses; identical masked instructions do not imply equal dynamic cost.
* i27 holds print_utf8 and Engine::apply entry addresses constant but Unicode
  still regresses. Entry-address movement is NOT necessary for regression.
* i29 holds those entry addresses constant, shrinks print_utf8 by 6.30%, and
  preserves Engine::apply's relocation-masked sequence, but Unicode still loses
  ~8.495% point throughput. Caller size reduction alone is NOT sufficient.
* i29's helper adds code outside the caller: caller-only size must not be called
  whole-executable shrinkage. Total cache/stall causes remain unmeasured.
* Fewer operations in separately instrumented code do not prove lower cost in
  the uninstrumented timing ELF. The decode-counter observation is not rescinded;
  its performance inference is rejected.
* Same binary temporal variation does not prove earlier effects fictitious or
  authorize post-hoc corrections. It makes controlled conditions and prospective
  same-build repeatability a prerequisite, not an optional decoration.
