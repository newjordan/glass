Iteration 18: reciprocal same-L3 hot-thread placement on the exact selected build
Status: reported research; not championship acceptance or runtime promotion.

Question and intervention
-------------------------
Earlier whole-die, reader-unpinned, producer-domain, cross-L3 reader split and
same-logical-CPU helper-contention experiments did not deliberately compare
reader/parser SMT siblings with different physical cores inside the same L3.
This experiment changes only own-parent pty-read/pty-parse affinity masks,
using the unchanged selected executable:
 d75cb26fb48120df841ff876ee110c1e45e5feb6c3996ddc90d01e681200805c.
No Rust edit, build, sampler, preload, pass-boundary hook, or producer pin.
bench/tput.py is unchanged: six workloads, DA1, 154x39, fastest of three.
Boundary /proc snapshots and affinity changes still make this an artificial
placement experiment, not an ordinary, completely unobserved terminal score.

Sysfs at capture identifies physical sibling pairs (0,16) and (1,17), all
sharing a 98304K L3 (CPUs 0-7,16-23). Each capture verifies this topology and
the exact binary. Arms (reader,parser):
 auto: unchanged shipping large-L3 masks
 s0=(0,16), s1=(1,17): siblings
 d01=(0,17), d10=(1,16): distinct physical cores, same L3.
Both contrasts use each selected CPU equally for each role. The producer and
all other thread masks are not modified by the wrapper. This is not a claim
that no other thread changed its own affinity.

Lifecycle and provenance
------------------------
Wrapper checks direct-parent executable and role identity/start_ticks, pins
once, executes unchanged payload, checks masks and producer mask at boundaries,
then restores both original masks independently, including partial failures.
Capture is complete only after successful payload and restoration. Runner
records exact source/executable/protocol and raw hashes, fresh directories,
actual planned order, and before/after source checks. Read-only analyzer
checks duplicate keys, shapes, rounded times/rates, identity, masks, topology,
restoration, hashes, actual time order and non-overlapping whole payloads.
Snapshots do not prove residency, migration counts, or unchanged masks between
boundaries. Names/start_ticks are Linux practical identities, not provenance
against an adversarial writer or same-tick task reuse.

Prespecified statistics
-----------------------
20 deterministic outer rounds per v2 series, five cyclic rotations followed
by their reversals, repeated twice. Each arm occupies each position four
times. Primary per-round ratio sqrt(d01*d10)/sqrt(s0*s1), then its median;
NOT a ratio of across-round medians. Outer round is the unit, n=20 per metric;
passes and reciprocal arms are not independent replicates. Exact two-sided
binomial sign tests, ties removed, Holm across the six workloads separately
for the primary family. Prespecified d/auto and s/auto six-workload families
are DESCRIPTIVE followups, not a rescue of a failed primary analysis.
Exact-binomial p-values condition on independent fair signs under the null;
deterministic balancing does not establish that assumption. Temporal load
can correlate rounds. No exact randomized-inference claim, no cross-series
or all-18-comparisons family guarantee, no either-run-significant rule.

Concrete new lead, and limits
-----------------------------
Under these shared-host conditions, distinct-core pins show a repeatable
SECONDARY ASCII d/auto contrast (~31-33%) in two separate v2 series, while
primary topology-family results vary. This is enough to prioritize controlled
placement research, not to ship fixed pins. The reciprocal design separates
CPU marginal choice from sibling pairing but not pure SMT from cache,
scheduling, simultaneous load, power/frequency or producer competition.
Each series would need its own prospective validation/benefit-regression
margins, not arbitrary pooling or selective significance.

Absolute auto ASCII medians: 222.8 and 250.9 MB/s; Unicode 93.4 and 94.55.
These differ markedly from earlier exact-build datasets. A post-confirmatory
host witness during replication records loadavg 36.30/37.91/32.90 and many busy
Lean compiler workers; ps percentages are lifetime averages, not instantaneous
occupancy. This establishes contemporaneous shared load, not the cause or
state of every timed pass. Do not normalize against old baselines or blame
all disagreement on proven host noise. No unrelated task was altered.
GPU-init thread transitions are observations; boundaries are around the whole
payload (including setup/geometry stabilization/generation), not timed passes.
They are not proof that GPU initialization overlapped the timed workload.

Fixed cores, unrestricted producer (can share these cores), external load,
no CPU isolation, deterministic order and boundary warm-state effects limit
transferability. There is no new Ghostty/startup/key-to-screen/CPU-memory
comparison. No measurement here closes the historical remaining Ghostty gaps.
