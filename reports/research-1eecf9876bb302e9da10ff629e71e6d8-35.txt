DECISION / MATERIAL NEW GROUND
Rejected an architectural runtime candidate before timing. Replacing the
synthetic-selection scroll observer with an output-only Term counter loses
height-only resize movement: shrinking the live grid can push rows into
history without any output scroll. All 76 independently expected resize cases
fail the candidate and pass the shipping runtime. Both fast/slow lanes fail,
so parser equivalence is not a sufficient semantic gate for this change.
Retained two passing cfg(test) regression tests, not the runtime candidate.

A second, independent finding: the SHIPPING runtime miscounts output after
setting GLASS_SCROLLBACK=8. After 12,000 repetitions of x CR LF on a four-row
screen, the correct primary scroll count is 11,997. Shipping reports 3 with
set_motion(None), or 10,664 with unknown picture extent Some(u64::MAX), in both
fast and slow lanes. Each isolated process uses the public configuration,
not just a test-only direct history mutation. The rejected candidate reports
11,997 in all four cases, but its resize regression prevents adoption. This
short-history defect is reported, NOT fixed. The diagnostic remains archived,
not installed as a failing or ignored production-source test.
