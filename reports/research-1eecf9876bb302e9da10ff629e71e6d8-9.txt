1. New hypotheses, independently tested

A. Consumer coalescing: append at most one already-queued producer handoff
via try_recv to the parser's existing 64-KiB buffer. Keep producer 16-KiB
chunks, two credits, immediate recycling, partial-tail retention and affinity.
This differs from previously rejected larger producer chunks, extra credits,
borrowed buffers and scheduler placement. Six reader tests passed.

B. Lazy frame cuts: yield complete synchronized-frame starts lazily, then
chain the final read boundary. Removes the ordinary read's boundary-vector
allocation without changing read size or parser. Seven reader/gate tests
passed, including direct-scan randomized cut equivalence.

C. Inline pieces: append-only splitter output abstraction, four-element
SmallVec in the production session, heap spill beyond four. No splitter
state transitions changed. Existing Vec byte-step reference retained.
Existing differential test passed over 600 random streams/read cuts.
A direct smallvec dependency reused its pinned transitive version; dependency,
lockfile, storage/API and temporary differential edits restored afterward.
No candidate combined any of A/B/C. All snapshots/patches remain explicit.

4. Important measurement limitation / new observations

Unchanged control ASCII rates ranged 64.1-1119.3 MB/s in A, 67.1-1115.7 in B,
65.8-1132.6 in C. Inline-piece candidate ranged 62.9-1116.2. In C the control
recorded 65.8 MB/s with .45 s terminal CPU in one session and 1099.2 MB/s /
.04 s in another. This is evidence of instability, not a proven causal
explanation, and not an excuse to filter results or claim a universal gain.

A lightweight /proc observer was added partway through C, after severe early
excursions. 176 one-second snapshots are retained; 25 sampled observations
for each Glass hot thread/arm all show masks 0-7,16-23; Ghostty original mask
0-31. No competitor affinity changed. Sparse snapshots cannot diagnose
producer placement, migration timing, blocked time or coherence. Observer
overhead is unmeasured and explicitly part of that run's limitations. No
environment or command-line data was collected. perf is absent from PATH,
perf_event_paranoid=2; no PMU trace or causal CPU explanation is claimed.
